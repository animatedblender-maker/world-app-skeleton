# What’s next (near future)

Yes — Matterya can reach “next level social” quality in the near future **as slices**, not as one overnight TikTok clone.

### Recommended order
1. **Ship text ANN live** — finish backfill + `VOYAGE_API_KEY` on Render + verify Home feels smarter  
2. **Butter-smooth clients** — standing quality bar on every iOS/web change  
3. **Cold start v1** — new users get sensible posts from country/hub priors + similar content (no custom ML training needed)  
4. **Creator clarity** — correct attribution (done in code), simple stats, clearer publish paths  
5. **Trust / safety loop** — report → review → action (keyword/LLM triage can come later)

### How Voyage fits the big picture
Voyage is the **understanding layer** (text → meaning → similar posts).  
MIND is the **decision layer** (events + ledger + rank).  
Creators, safety, and smooth video are the **product layer**.

All three together feel like a real platform. Embeddings alone do not.
